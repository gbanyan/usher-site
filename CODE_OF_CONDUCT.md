# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project belongs to a community of people living with combined vision and
hearing loss, their families, and the clinicians, educators and volunteers who
support them. We are committed to making participation here a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of disability, age, body size, visible or
invisible difference, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being welcoming, patient and kind
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community this project serves
- **Communicating accessibly.** Describe images you post. Do not rely on colour
  alone to make a point. Prefer plain language. Assume some readers use a screen
  reader, a magnifier, or a braille display.

Behaviour that will not be tolerated:

- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Condescension about a person's disability, or dismissing an accessibility
  problem as unimportant because it does not affect you
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues,
pull requests and discussions — and when an individual is representing the
project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the association
through the channels listed at
[www.usher.org.tw/contact](https://www.usher.org.tw/contact). All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards
and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. They have the right
to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the
project's leadership.

All reporters' privacy and security will be respected.
